# Fixed industrial measurement models

Nine channels are defined in each configs/sensing preset: process temperature (BW-01), process pressure (BW-01), normalized load (DR-01), RMS vibration feature (SUP-01), surface thermometers (BW-01 and DR-01), pipe process temperature (SP-01), pressure (SP-02), and RMS vibration feature (DR-01). VL-01/REF-01 and most surface regions are not instrumented. An asset association does not imply complete spatial sensing coverage.

Pressure/load/process temperature read the actual common plant process state, not independent random series or hidden equilibrium references. Process pressure is shared by this simplified plant rather than a solved spatial pressure network. Surface thermometers find the closest triangle on their configured asset to a world mounting location and barycentrically interpolate its current vertex field. This is an idealized fixed-location contact/attachment approximation, not a mechanical probe-contact model. Public measurement_source explicitly separates process, surface, operating_state and phenomenological values.

Vibration is a documented phenomenological RMS indicator (m/s2): max(0, base + load_gain×load + seeded Gaussian variation). It is not an accelerometer waveform or structural dynamics solution. Neither geometric nor thermal faults automatically produce abnormal vibration; there is no hidden label-to-signal coupling. Defaults are 0.15 m/s2 base and 0.2 m/s2 per unit load; L0 variation is zero, other presets use 0.01 m/s2. Reference feature variation and measurement uncertainty use independent named random streams.

Each channel has a positive sampling interval; scheduled samples start at t=0 and include the final time only when on-grid. Defaults range from 1 to 10 s. Clock jitter here means actual sample-time jitter, drawn independently and clipped into the scenario bounds; both physical sampling and acquired_at use that actual time. The exported timestamp is not an unrelated wall clock or a silently corrupted plant reference. sequence_number identifies the scheduled sample even when some samples are absent.

Measurement noise adds configured-unit Gaussian variation and systematic bias, optional quantization and outliers, then clips to the instrument range. Scalar uncertainty combines measurement sigma and quantum/sqrt(12); it does not pretend to include unknown systematic bias or structural-model fidelity. Saturation is marked degraded. Load is a fraction in [0,1], pressure Pa, temperature degC, vibration RMS m/s2. Missing samples are omitted instead of represented by invented values.

Delivery has independent dropout, finite interruption windows, nonnegative modeled delay with bounded jitter and optional retransmission. received_at always follows acquired_at. JSONL/CSV are ordered by receipt; records retain original acquisition time and sequence. A lower sequence arriving after a higher one is marked out_of_order; retransmissions keep the same observation ID/sequence and are marked duplicate. A delay longer than the sample interval is marked late. This is a transport flag, not an evidence freshness or severity decision. Phase 4 fusion and Phase 5 network integration remain unimplemented.

Private references retain expected attempts, actual physical/reference feature value, omitted samples and measurement errors. Validation counts unique delivered IDs for completeness and handles duplicate messages separately. The canonical export is validated SensorObservation JSONL; CSV is a companion table. Known installation descriptors are exported separately without truth values. Batch datasets are replay-ready but no MQTT/OPC UA broker, sensor-corruption fault injector, alarm logic or condition classification is implemented.
