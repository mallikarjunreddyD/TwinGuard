# Evidence semantics

FusionEvidence preserves source/algorithm/version, provenance, acquisition/receipt/processing/availability times, clock quality, asset and region association, measurement scope/basis, numeric values/units, engineering uncertainty, calibration/registration/coverage status, source lineage and quality reasons. Positive and negative findings require good calibrated valid evidence; inconclusive, invalid and unavailable are separate findings. No confidence probability is invented.

B3.1 positives are qualified localized wall-relative changes. Negative B3.1 evidence only describes no significant localized change at achieved sensitivity/coverage. It does not negate global motion, support movement, unseen areas or later faults. Positive regions require qualified contributing patches and calibration support; unqualified raw patch values never become valid evidence. Failed registration yields unavailable evidence; L2 abstention remains inconclusive. Asset-level statuses preserve unavailable support evidence. Original B3 remains a selectable alternate producer and is not independent confirmation of the same scans.

Thermal results are numerical apparent-temperature residuals. Saturation/quantitative pixel counts, model and capture references and image bounds are preserved. Image-only regions have no assigned asset and contribute only to inspection-context advisories. They cannot satisfy physical overlap rules. Even an asset-associated ThermalResult without published world bounds cannot establish 3D region overlap. Apparent radiometry is not an independently calibrated physical surface temperature.

IoT residual evidence retains the measured value, estimated reference, residual, source quality and model applicability. Installed surface thermometers have surface-temperature hypotheses distinct from bulk process temperature. Learned residuals do not become critical events. A separate raw-observation adapter evaluates only explicitly configured engineering limits, known installation/asset, matching units, instrument range, calibration, quality and timestamp conditions. Unconfigured limits are unsupported; site-template.yaml intentionally supplies none.

Different algorithms/versions on one capture pair and raw/residual indicators from one sample remain dependent. Algorithm preference selects alternatives without counting extra confirmations; every original record remains in state. Shared references/groups are conservatively treated as dependent, with optional configured dependence-group overrides. Separate sensor IDs do not overcome a declared common signal/calibration group. No false-positive probabilities are multiplied.

Assessment availability is not acquisition time. ProcessingRecord must bind a specific source and record receipt, processing and availability. The prepare CLI uses the actual offline seal completion time and defaults clock quality to unknown. E3 explicitly uses a separately labeled simulated_replay contract: raw ingestion 0.05 s and operating/thermal processing 0.25 s after receipt, measured geometry computation after receipt, synchronized synthetic clocks. These assumptions do not measure a deployed service's latency.

Recency windows are hazard-specific; joint rules additionally bound acquisition separation including clock uncertainty. Newer available samples take precedence over older arrivals. Invalid clocks, future/unavailable/stale evidence cannot become current support. Inverted source timestamps are schema errors, not silently repaired timestamps. Historical source IDs may still support unresolved advisory history; they are explicitly separated from current corroboration. Repair records invalidate pre-repair geometry for current use while retaining history.

Green is scoped policy coverage, never a whole-boiler safety certificate. Missing coverage and qualified abnormal channels awaiting activation cannot become green. No normal process reading vetoes a different geometric hypothesis; no localized geometric negative vetoes a critical pressure event. Unknown coverage remains visible alongside credible red escalation. Consumers must display scope, confidence and missing evidence with severity.
