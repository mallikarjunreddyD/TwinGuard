# Hazard policies

configs/fusion/hazards.yaml is an illustrative simulation policy. It declares accepted asset types, installations/instrument ranges and calibration status, scoped green requirements, hazard rules, freshness/coincidence limits, physical compatibility, independent-source requirements, actions, persistence and recovery. FusionPolicy validates unique IDs, asset/type references and feasible distinct-modality counts. All qualified findings require good valid calibrated evidence; this common minimum cannot be relaxed by an unvalidated policy field.

The example critical pressure limit is 1,150,000 Pa: the fixed synthetic model's nominal full-load pressure envelope reaches 1,100,000 Pa and the example adds a 50,000 Pa reserve. The critical temperature example is 240 degC: nominal full-load temperature reaches 230 degC with a 10 degC reserve. These are illustrative research parameters, not equipment/site/statutory limits or approvals. An early development smoke used an inappropriate 180 degC example at the nominal reference; its false reds were preserved and corrected using the fixed nominal envelope before fresh final seeds. See phase4/development_policy_review.json.

Critical raw pressure/temperature crossings use their own rule path, 10 s freshness, known calibrated instrument, valid range/units, good quality and adequate clocks. They may raise red immediately with one credible source even when LiDAR is stale/missing and the operating model is out of range. Operating residuals instead raise amber. No shutdown, valve, certified protection or notification transport is implemented.

Localized geometry produces amber, requires qualified registration/calibration/coverage and persists until recorded repair or explicit review. Thermal-only evidence produces an amber inspection-context advisory when image-only. Two independent recent geometry/thermal findings require compatible published physical regions for the stronger red combination; image-only cannot satisfy it. Geometry/process-temperature combination requires compatible asset context and timing and remains amber. Shared sources/calibration never count twice.

BW-01 green coverage requires current pressure/temperature limit evidence, pressure operating-reference coverage and a supported localized inspection. A positive required channel is covered abnormal evidence, not mislabeled missing; it cannot yield green while activation/persistence is pending. Global/support evidence is not implied by local coverage. Other assets without normal-coverage requirements remain unknown by F1, even if no abnormality is reported. Configuring green for more assets requires explicit monitoring requirements.

configs/fusion/site-template.yaml is conservative and intentionally unconfigured for critical limits/calibration. Actual deployment requires site-approved engineering limits, verified sensor installation/calibration, clock validation and authorized operator procedures. The engine trusts verified public producer metadata; it is not a hostile-process security boundary or a certified industrial safety system.
